# AQSkill Technologies 2022 Sales/Profit Report

## Project Title
AQSkill Technologies – 2022 Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

## Project Objectives
- To analyze the overall sales and profit performance of AQSkill Technologies.
- To identify the most and least profitable sub-categories, segments, and product categories.
- To examine sales quantity trends over time and regional contribution.
- To provide clear, actionable insights that support better pricing, product, and market decisions.

## Dataset Used
Dataset: AQSkill Technologies Sales Data  
Contains order-level data including sales, profit, quantity, discount, segment, category, sub-category, region, and ship mode.

## Key Questions Explored
1. What is the total sales, profit, quantity sold, and total discount given?
2. Which sub-categories generate the highest and lowest profit?
3. How has sales quantity trended over time?
4. Which customer segments contribute the most to profit?
5. How is profit distributed across product categories (Technology, Office Supplies, Furniture)?
6. Which regions contribute the most to overall business performance?
7. How do different segments perform across major product categories?

## Process
1. **Data Cleaning and Preparation**
   - Standardized category, sub-category, segment, and region names.
   - Handled missing or inconsistent values.
   - Created aggregated views for profit by sub-category, segment, and category.

2. **Data Analysis**
   - Calculated total sales, profit, quantity, and discount.
   - Ranked sub-categories by profit contribution.
   - Analyzed quantity trends over time.
   - Examined profit distribution by segment, category, and region.

## Dashboard Building
- Built an interactive sales and profit dashboard using **Google Looker Studio**.
- Included key performance indicators (Sales, Quantity, Profit, Discount).
- Created visualizations for Profit by Sub-Category, Quantity Trend over Time, Profit by Segment, Category vs Segment sales table, Regional performance (treemap), and Profit by Category.
- Added a Ship Mode filter for flexible analysis.

## Recommendations and Insights
1. **Top Profitable Sub-Categories**  
   Copiers (258.6K), Phones (216.7K), and Bookcases (161.9K) are the highest profit generators.  
   ➔ Prioritize inventory, marketing, and upselling efforts on these high-margin sub-categories.

2. **Loss-Making Sub-Category**  
   Tables recorded a loss of -64.1K.  
   ➔ Review pricing, discount strategy, or consider discontinuing or repositioning the Tables product line.

3. **Segment Performance**  
   Consumer segment contributes the highest profit share (51.1%), followed by Corporate (30.1%) and Home Office (18.8%).  
   ➔ Strengthen Consumer-focused campaigns while developing targeted strategies to grow the Corporate and Home Office segments.

4. **Category Profitability**  
   Technology leads with 45.2% of total profit, followed by Office Supplies (35.3%) and Furniture (19.4%).  
   ➔ Continue investing in Technology products while improving margins in the Furniture category.

5. **Quantity Growth Trend**  
   Sales quantity shows a clear upward trend, reaching 21.1K in the latest period.  
   ➔ Maintain momentum with consistent demand planning and inventory readiness.

6. **Regional Contribution**  
   Asia Pacific and Europe appear as major contributing regions.  
   ➔ Focus expansion and resource allocation on high-performing regions while evaluating growth potential in LATAM and Africa.

## Conclusion
This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of AQSkill Technologies’ sales and profit performance. It clearly highlights high-performing products, profitable customer segments, and regional strengths, while also flagging underperforming areas such as the Tables sub-category. The interactive design enables stakeholders to explore the data easily and make informed decisions to improve profitability and drive sustainable growth.
